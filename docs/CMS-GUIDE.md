# Editing your site — a guide for Daniel

Everything on your portfolio — words, pictures, colours, projects, blog posts and your resume —
can be changed from your browser, without touching any code.

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://daniel-lotsu.vercel.app/cms**.
2. Enter your email (**danielajayi100@gmail.com**) and your password, then press **Unlock**.

Change the starting password the first time you sign in (see **Settings** below). If you get it
wrong several times in a row, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes to keep the account safe.

## How the screen is laid out

- **Left:** the list of things you can edit — each part of the page, plus Theme, Inbox and
  Settings. A small **yellow dot** means that part has changes that aren't live yet.
- **Middle:** the editor for the part you picked.
- **Right:** a **live preview** of your site, showing your changes as you type. Switch between
  **Desktop** and **Mobile** at the top. (On a small screen the preview is hidden.)
- **Top:** whether your changes are saved, **Undo**, **View site**, and **Publish**.

## Editing and saving

Just type. Your changes are **saved automatically** as a draft a moment after you stop typing —
the top bar says "Draft saved".

A draft is private: **visitors don't see it until you publish.** You can edit over several days
and publish when you're happy.

If something can't be saved (for example a link that isn't a proper web address), the field turns
red with a message explaining what to fix, and the top bar says so.

### Lists

Projects, stats, facts, jobs, blog posts and so on are lists:

- Click an item to open it; click again to fold it away.
- Use the arrows to move it up or down, the copy button to duplicate it, and the bin to delete it
  (you'll be asked to confirm).
- **Add …** at the bottom adds a new item.

### Bold text

In your **About** bio, put `**double asterisks**` around words to make them bold, like
`I build **reliable systems**`.

### Pictures and your resume PDF

Press **Upload** and pick a file, or paste a link to an image that's already online.

- Pictures: PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG or AVIF, up to 5 MB.
- Resume: a PDF, up to 5 MB. Once uploaded, the **Download PDF** button appears on your resume.
- **Remove** clears the picture (the placeholder shows instead).
- Fill in the **description** fields next to project covers, your portrait and blog covers. They
  describe the picture for people who use screen readers, and help search engines.

### Blog posts

The **first** post in the list is shown as the big "Latest" post. Each post has:

- a short title (for lists) and a full title (for the article page),
- a **Link (URL)** — the end of its web address, e.g. `my-first-year` becomes
  `…/blog/my-first-year`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, and don't change it once
  people have shared the post,
- article content made of **blocks**: choose **Paragraph** for normal text or **Code** for code
  (the code language label appears above the code).

## Publishing

When you're ready, press **Publish** at the top. The number on it tells you how many parts have
unpublished changes. Your live site updates within a few seconds — no need to wait for anything
else.

## Undo, history and resetting

- **Undo** (top bar, or Ctrl+Z when you're not typing in a box) reverses your recent changes one
  step at a time, for as long as the CMS is open.
- **Version history** (bottom of each editor) lists the last 20 times that part was published.
  **Restore** puts that version back into your draft so you can check it and publish it again.
- **Reset section** (top of each editor) puts that part back to the original content. It only
  changes your draft, and you can undo it.

## Theme & layout

- Pick one of five **accent colours**, or type your own in **Custom colour**. If your colour
  would be hard to read on the dark background, you'll see a warning — choose something
  brighter.
- Choose whether projects show as a **grid** or a **list**.
- Turn the **scrolling tech strip** and the **starlight background** on or off.

Theme changes are part of your draft too — press **Publish** to make them live.

## Inbox

Messages from your site's contact form arrive here, newest first, with a count in the sidebar.

- **Reply** opens your email app with the sender's address filled in.
- **Mark read / Mark unread** and **Delete** (you'll be asked to confirm; deleting can't be
  undone).

If email notifications are switched on, you'll also get an email for each new message.

## Settings

- **Change password** — enter your current password and your new one twice (at least 10
  characters). Other devices are signed out.
- **Export backup** — downloads all your content as a file. Keep one somewhere safe before big
  changes.
- **Import backup** — loads a backup file into your draft (it's checked first; nothing goes live
  until you publish).
- **Reset everything** — puts all content and theme settings back to the originals in your
  draft. Your inbox and password are kept.
- **Sign out** — ends your session. You're also signed out automatically after 12 hours.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't save"** — check your internet connection; it retries on your next change.
- **A field is red** — read the message under it; the part won't save until it's fixed.
- **The live site hasn't changed** — make sure you pressed Publish and that it says
  "Published", then refresh the page.
- Anything else: contact your developer with a screenshot of the screen.
